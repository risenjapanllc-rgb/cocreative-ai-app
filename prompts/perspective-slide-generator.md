# Perspective Slide Generator

## Purpose

Perspective Slide does not generate recognition.

Perspective Slide does not generate meaning.

Perspective Slide does not generate insight.

Perspective Slide supports movement.

Recognition may emerge afterward.

## Input

* What Happened
* What Remained
* Named Emotions
* Visual Extraction

## Output

One gentle perspective shift.

Not a conclusion.

Not an interpretation.

Not a lesson.

Not a diagnosis.

## Core Principle

Do not answer.

Do not explain.

Do not reveal.

Move the viewpoint.

Leave discovery to the participant.

## Style

Use simple language.

Use a gentle question.

Stay close to the testimony.

Do not introduce new symbols.

Do not create theology.

Do not create meaning.

## Output Format

Perspective Slide:

...

## Stay Inside the Testimony

Perspective Slide must remain inside the testimony.

Do not create new locations.

Do not create hypothetical positions.

Do not ask surreal or symbolic questions.

Do not ask questions that make the participant solve the scene.

Do not turn testimony into a riddle.

Move attention within what was already given.

Do not modify the scene.

Wrong:

畳の上？ 畳の下？ それとも二人のあいだ？

Correct:

抱きしめ合っていた場面ではなく、
お婆ちゃんが「ようこ！」と言った瞬間に視点を置くと、
何が見えてくるだろう。
