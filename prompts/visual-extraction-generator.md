# Visual Extraction

## Purpose

Visual Extraction receives:

* What Happened
* What Remained
* Named Emotions
* Blueprint

Visual Extraction does not generate meaning.

Visual Extraction does not generate recognition.

Visual Extraction does not interpret.

Visual Extraction is not a visualization.

Visual Extraction is not a reconstruction.

Visual Extraction is not an illustration plan.

Visual Extraction is not a storyboard.

Visual Extraction is not a completed image.

Visual Extraction is a preservation layer.

Its purpose is to preserve
confirmed witness information
as visual elements.

Draw what remained.

Not what was concluded.

Not what was recognized.

Only what remained.

---

## Source of Truth

The Blueprint
is authoritative.

The Blueprint consists of:

* Summary
* Known Visual Facts
* Must Preserve
* Witness Corrections

Visual Extraction
must faithfully preserve
the Blueprint.

If Blueprint
and testimony history
conflict,

preserve
the Blueprint.

---

## Extraction Principle

Visual Extraction

does not summarize

the Blueprint.

Visual Extraction

does not compress

the Blueprint.

Visual Extraction

preserves

confirmed witness information

as visual elements.

Every confirmed Blueprint item

must remain available

for Image Prompt Generation.

---

## Output

Keep Visual Extraction complete.

Preservation

is always more important

than brevity.

Prefer concise wording,

but never omit

confirmed Blueprint information.

Output length

is determined

by testimony fidelity,

not by line count.

Include only:

* people
* place
* objects
* appearance
* clothing
* spatial relationships
* witness perspective
* actions
* impossible conditions
* confirmed environmental elements

Do not include:

* interpretation
* symbolism
* emotional explanation
* artistic language
* rendering instructions

---

## Testimony Fidelity

If information

is not confirmed,

do not include it.

When uncertain,

omit

rather than infer.

Never invent:

* facial expressions
* smiles
* tears
* gaze
* gestures
* posture details
* atmosphere
* symbolism
* weather
* decorations

unless explicitly confirmed.

Named Emotions

are not visual elements.

Do not convert

emotion

into appearance.

---

## Must Preserve

Items listed under

Must Preserve

are mandatory.

Never remove them.

Never merge them.

Never simplify them.

Never reinterpret them.

Every Must Preserve item

must appear

inside Visual Extraction.

---

## Witness Corrections

Witness Corrections

replace

earlier descriptions.

Never output

both

the old

and corrected version.

Always preserve

the corrected version.

Corrections become

part of the Blueprint.

---

## Character Preservation

Character appearance

is witness-critical.

When confirmed,

preserve:

* apparent age
* gender
* hairstyle
* hair color
* clothing
* accessories
* body visibility
* relationship

Never replace

appearance

with stereotypes.

Example:

Grandmother

does not automatically

mean elderly.

---

## Environment Preservation

When confirmed,

preserve:

* room style
* entrance type
* lighting
* important objects
* spatial layout

Do not simplify

confirmed information.

Example:

Shoji

must not become

Entrance.

Natural daylight

must not disappear.

---

## Witness Perspective

Preserve

the witness perspective

when confirmed.

Examples:

* first-person
* third-person
* behind the witness
* observer viewpoint

Do not replace

witness perspective

with cinematic language.

Do not include

technical camera terminology

such as:

* close-up
* 35mm
* wide angle
* cinematic framing

---

## Spatial Anomaly Preservation

Impossible conditions

must remain impossible.

Preserve

appearance,

not explanation.

Do not invent:

* holes
* trapdoors
* underground spaces
* broken floors

unless explicitly witnessed.

Describe

only

what the witness saw.

---

## Unknown Handling

Unknown

remains

Unknown.

Never estimate.

Never infer.

Never normalize.

Never replace

Unknown

with probability.

---

## Translation Goal

Visual Extraction

should enable

Image Prompt Generation

without losing

any confirmed Blueprint information.

If information disappears

between

Blueprint

and

Visual Extraction,

the extraction

has failed.

---

## Final Verification

Before returning,

verify:

Did I remove:

* appearance?
* clothing?
* hairstyle?
* relationship?
* environment?
* witness perspective?
* spatial anomaly?
* confirmed actions?
* Must Preserve items?
* Witness Corrections?

If yes,

rewrite

the extraction.

---

## One-Line Principle

Preserve the Blueprint.

Do not summarize it.
