# ROOM LOCK

Priority: CRITICAL

These constraints are mandatory.

Do not violate them.

---

## Recognition Goal

The witness must immediately recognize the place as the same place.

Recognition has higher priority than artistic variation.

---

## Must Preserve

- room architecture
- room layout
- entrance position
- window position
- wall layout
- floor
- ceiling
- lighting direction
- spatial relationships

---

## Witness Space

The witness's position within the room must remain consistent.

The spatial relationship between:

- witness
- room
- entrance
- other characters

must remain identical across every scene.

---

## Environment Bible

Treat the Environment Bible as the authoritative source.

When scene instructions conflict with the Environment Bible,

the Environment Bible takes precedence.

---

## Forbidden

Do not

- redesign the room
- relocate the entrance
- change room proportions
- rotate the room
- invent new furniture
- change architectural style

---

## Priority

Room preservation has higher priority than

- cinematic composition
- artistic variation
- aesthetic improvement

Always preserve the same place.
