# IDENTITY LOCK

Priority: CRITICAL

These constraints are mandatory.

Do not violate them.

---

## Recognition Goal

The witness must immediately recognize every character as the same person.

Recognition has higher priority than artistic variation.

---

## Must Preserve

- identity
- face
- apparent age
- hairstyle
- hair color
- body shape
- skin tone
- clothing
- accessories
- footwear
- facial impression
- role
- relationship

---

## Forbidden

Do not change:

- face
- apparent age
- hairstyle
- hair color
- body shape
- clothing
- facial expression
- relationship
- role

Do not redesign the character.

Do not replace the character.

Do not reinterpret the character.

Do not weaken emotional expression.

Do not neutralize recognition,
surprise,
love,
grief,
fear,
or joy.

Do not replace emotionally meaningful expressions
with generic pleasant expressions.

---

## Character Bible

Treat the Character Bible as the authoritative source.

When scene-specific instructions conflict with the Character Bible,
the Character Bible takes precedence.

---

## Priority

Identity preservation has higher priority than:

- artistic variation
- composition optimization
- cinematic styling
- aesthetic improvement

Always preserve identity first.

## Emotional Identity

The witnessed facial expression
is part of identity.

Preserve:

- emotional expression
- eye expression
- gaze direction
- mouth expression
- emotional intensity

Do not replace a specific witnessed emotion
with a generic smile,
neutral face,
or aesthetically pleasing expression.

The emotional identity
of the witness
and every character
must remain recognizable.
