# SCENE CONTINUITY LOCK

Priority: CRITICAL

These constraints are mandatory.

Do not violate them.

---

## Recognition Goal

Every scene must feel like it belongs
to the same witnessed world.

The witness must recognize:

- the same people
- the same place
- the same spatial relationships
- the same emotional continuity

across every scene.

Continuity has higher priority
than visual variety.

---

## Same World Rule

Do not regenerate each scene
as a new world.

Every scene is a continuation
of the same Witness World.

Preserve:

- character identity
- apparent age
- hairstyle
- clothing
- room identity
- room layout
- entrance position
- window position
- lighting direction
- camera relationship
- spatial relationships
- emotional progression

---

## Environment Continuity

Do not create a similar room.

Create the same room.

Do not:

- redesign architecture
- rotate the room
- move entrances
- move windows
- add new furniture
- remove witness-critical features
- change lighting direction
- change the apparent time of day

---

## Character Continuity

Do not create similar characters.

Create the same characters.

Preserve:

- face
- age
- hair
- clothing
- body shape
- role
- relationship
- emotional identity

A character must not become
a new version of themselves
between scenes.

---

## Camera Continuity

Do not change camera position
unless the Director explicitly requires it.

Preserve:

- viewpoint
- camera height
- camera direction
- distance relationship
- axis of observation

A camera change must never make
the witnessed world appear to be
a different place.

---

## Action Continuity

Each scene may show a different moment,
but it must belong to the same event sequence.

Do not collapse events.

Do not skip required transitions.

Do not turn a sequence into
generic emotional imagery.

Preserve the order of witnessed events.

---

## Emotional Continuity

Emotions may develop across scenes,
but they must remain faithful
to the testimony.

Do not neutralize emotion.

Do not replace a specific witnessed emotion
with a generic pleasant expression.

Do not make characters emotionally inconsistent
between scenes.

---

## Forbidden

Do not:

- create a new room for each scene
- create a new face for each scene
- change clothing between scenes
- change lighting without testimony
- change camera axis without instruction
- redesign the setting for variety
- alter relationships between characters
- make scenes feel unrelated

---

## Priority

Scene continuity has higher priority than:

- visual variety
- cinematic variety
- artistic exploration
- aesthetic improvement
- dramatic reinterpretation

Always preserve the same witnessed world.
