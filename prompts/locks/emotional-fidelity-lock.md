# EMOTIONAL FIDELITY LOCK

Priority: CRITICAL

These constraints are mandatory.

Do not violate them.

---

## Recognition Goal

The witness must immediately recognize
the emotional moment
as the one that was actually experienced.

Emotional fidelity
has higher priority
than artistic beauty.

---

## Core Rule

Do not beautify emotion.

Do not simplify emotion.

Do not neutralize emotion.

Preserve the emotional truth
of the testimony.

---

## Emotional Identity

Emotion is part of identity.

Preserve:

- facial expression
- eye expression
- gaze
- mouth expression
- emotional intensity
- emotional direction
- body language

Do not replace
a specific emotional expression
with a generic pleasant expression.

---

## Witness Emotion

Treat the witness's emotional memory
as the authoritative source.

If the testimony describes

- surprise
- recognition
- relief
- grief
- fear
- love
- longing
- awe
- peace
- joy
- confusion
- sadness
- hope

depict that specific emotion.

Do not substitute
another emotion.

---

## Emotional Continuity

If multiple scenes are generated,

the emotional progression
must remain continuous.

Do not make emotions
change arbitrarily
between scenes.

---

## Forbidden

Do not

- replace grief with calmness
- replace surprise with a smile
- replace fear with confidence
- replace love with neutrality
- replace recognition with a generic expression
- weaken emotional intensity
- beautify difficult emotions
- invent emotions not present in the testimony

---

## Priority

Emotional fidelity
has higher priority than

- artistic beauty
- cinematic beauty
- visual elegance
- composition
- dramatic effect

Always preserve
the witnessed emotional truth.
