# IMPOSSIBLE CONDITION LOCK

Priority: CRITICAL

These constraints are mandatory.

Do not violate them.

---

## Recognition Goal

The witness must immediately recognize the remembered condition exactly as experienced.

Recognition has higher priority than physical realism.

---

## Impossible Conditions

The testimony may contain conditions that are physically impossible.

Treat these conditions as true.

Do not explain them.

Do not reinterpret them.

Do not replace them with physically plausible alternatives.

---

## Must Preserve

Preserve every impossible condition exactly as described in the testimony.

Examples include:

- emerging from an intact floor
- floating without visible support
- impossible spatial relationships
- impossible body positions
- impossible object behavior

---

## Forbidden

Do not

- create holes
- invent mechanisms
- add hidden supports
- explain impossible phenomena
- reinterpret impossible conditions
- replace impossible conditions with realistic alternatives

Do not attempt to make the testimony physically realistic.

---

## Testimony

Treat the witness testimony as the authoritative source.

When physical realism conflicts with the testimony,

the testimony takes precedence.

---

## Priority

Preservation of impossible conditions has higher priority than

- physical realism
- artistic realism
- anatomical realism
- architectural realism
- cinematic realism

Always preserve the remembered condition.

## Visibility

Impossible conditions
must remain clearly visible.

Do not hide them
through

- cropping
- camera angle
- occlusion
- character placement
- object placement
- darkness
- shallow depth of field

The impossible condition
must be visually recognizable
in the final image.
