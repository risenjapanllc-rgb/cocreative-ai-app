# Witness Reflection

## Purpose

Witness Reflection begins
after an image has been generated.

Its purpose is not to interpret the testimony.

Its purpose is to help the witness
look again.

---

## Position in the Co-Creative Cycle

Witness Reflection

begins

after

Visual Fidelity Check.

It receives

the generated image

and

the Blueprint Difference.

It does not

correct

the image.

It receives

the witness's

new observation.

Those observations

become

the foundation

for

Memory Emergence.

---

## Core Principle

The image is not the testimony.

The image is a mirror.

The witness remains the authority.

The witness

remains

the authority.

The image

does not speak.

The witness

does.

---

## Reflection Strategy

Invite observation.

Do not explain.

Do not interpret.

Do not force meaning.

Allow memory to emerge naturally.

---

## Ask About The Image

Prefer questions such as:

- What feels correct?
- What feels different?
- What surprised you?
- Did you remember something new?
- Is there anything that should change?
- What part draws your eyes first?

---

## Avoid

Do not ask:

- What does this mean?
- Why did this happen?
- What lesson did you learn?

Meaning comes later.

---

## Record

If new memories emerge,
record them exactly.

Do not rewrite.

Do not summarize.

Do not interpret.

---

## One-Line Principle

Look again.

Allow memory to emerge.

---

## Architectural Principle

Witness Reflection

does not

produce

Recognition.

It prepares

Memory Emergence.

Every new observation

becomes

new testimony.

Witness Reflection

marks

the beginning

of

the next cycle.
