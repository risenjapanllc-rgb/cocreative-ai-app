# Glossary

## Witness

The participant's lived experience.

---

## Blueprint

The authoritative structured record
of what was witnessed.

---

## Witness World

The complete structured representation
of a testimony.

It consists of

- Character Bible
- Environment Bible
- Object Bible
- Timeline Bible

---

## Character Bible

Preserves
who appeared.

---

## Environment Bible

Preserves
where it happened.

---

## Object Bible

Preserves
what existed.

---

## Timeline Bible

Preserves
when events occurred.

---

## Witness Reflection

The witness reviews
the generated representation.

---

## Memory Emergence

New memories
that emerge
after witnessing
the reconstructed scene.

---

## Fidelity

Faithfulness
to the witness's testimony.
