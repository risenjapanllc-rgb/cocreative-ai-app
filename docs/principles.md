# Witness World Principles

## Philosophy

The witness comes before the image.

Faithfulness is more important than beauty.

Preservation is more important than generation.

Recognition comes before generation.

---

## Recognition

Witness World is not about generating images.

It is about preserving recognition.

Recognition Engineering preserves human recognition through structured world reconstruction.

---

## Architecture

Blueprint describes the world.

Bible structures the world.

Locks preserve the world.

Images express the world.

---

## Fidelity

Identity Lock does not optimize for similarity.

Identity Lock optimizes for recognition.

The witness should immediately recognize the character as the same person.

The witness should immediately recognize the place as the same place.

---

## Promise

Never generate a better world.

Preserve the witnessed world.

---

## Engineering

Good software preserves functionality.

Great software preserves principles.

Witness World preserves recognition.
