The project is no longer only documenting design principles.

---

## Remaining Problems

Several important constraints were still violated.

- The witness was no longer emerging naturally from the tatami.
- Camera position drifted between scenes.
- Scene 3 recreated a visible hole in the floor.
- Spatial constraints were treated as suggestions rather than mandatory rules.

This indicates that simply describing a lock is not sufficient.

Image models require constraints that are interpreted as mandatory rather than descriptive.

---

## Discovery

A new design insight emerged today.

Identity Lock is not merely documentation.

It is an executable constraint.

However, successful recognition preservation requires more than writing rules.

The constraints themselves must be expressed in a form that image models interpret as non-negotiable.

This became the foundation of Recognition Engineering.

---

## Next Step

Possible structure:

The objective is not simply to describe the witness's world.

The objective is to preserve witness recognition.
