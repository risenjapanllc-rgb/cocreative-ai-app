# Scene Bible

## Purpose

Scene Bible preserves

how the witnessed world

is divided

into scenes.

It does not generate

new events.

It only organizes

the witness's testimony

into faithful scenes.

---

## Source of Truth

Scene Bible receives

- Blueprint
- Visual Extraction
- Character Bible
- Environment Bible
- Composition Bible
- Object Bible

Blueprint

is authoritative.

If sources conflict,

Blueprint wins.

---

## Scene Extraction

Extract

only scenes

that are explicitly

or necessarily implied

by the testimony.

Never invent

new scenes.

---

## Preserve

For every scene

preserve

- scene number
- title
- purpose
- participating characters
- environment
- objects
- actions
- dialogue
- witness viewpoint
- emotional focus

---

## Scene Continuity

Every scene

belongs

to the same

Witness World.

Preserve

- same characters
- same environment
- same composition
- same objects

unless

the testimony

describes change.

---

## Scene Order

Preserve

the witnessed order.

Never

rearrange scenes.

Never

merge

or split scenes

without evidence.

---

## Forbidden

Do not create

dramatic scenes.

Do not add

flashbacks,

dream sequences,

symbolic scenes,

or cinematic transitions.

Only preserve

the witnessed events.

---
