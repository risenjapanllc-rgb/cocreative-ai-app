# Witness Reflection Discoveries

## Discovery

Participant language often expands
after being faithfully received.

Example:

Participant:

大好きだったお婆ちゃん

AI:

大好きなお婆ちゃんだったんですね。

Result:

Participant shared additional memories.

---

## Discovery

Receiving often creates
more dialogue than reflection.
