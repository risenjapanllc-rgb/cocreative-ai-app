# Testimony Quality Standard

## Core Principle

A testimony representation is not judged primarily by beauty.

It is judged by fidelity.

Beauty is optional.

Fidelity is required.

---

## Fidelity Before Beauty

The purpose of Visual Testimony is not:

- to improve the testimony
- to beautify the testimony
- to dramatize the testimony
- to make the testimony more emotional
- to make the testimony more meaningful

The purpose is:

- to preserve the testimony faithfully

---

## Example

If the testimony is beautiful,

the representation may be beautiful.

If the testimony is disturbing,

the representation may be disturbing.

If the testimony is strange,

the representation may remain strange.

The testimony should not be normalized.

The testimony should not be beautified.

The testimony should not be repaired.

---

## Testimony Preservation

Do not replace unusual elements
with more common interpretations.

Example:

Testimony:

The wife's upper body emerged from the tatami.

Wrong:

- a hole in the floor
- a trapdoor
- kneeling in the floor

Correct:

- the wife's upper body emerges from the tatami

No explanation.

No normalization.

No correction.

---

## Fidelity Questions

Before outputting a representation, ask:

1. Was anything added?

2. Was anything removed?

3. Was anything normalized?

4. Was anything explained that was not explained in the testimony?

5. Would the witness say:

   "Yes.
   That is what I saw."

If not,

the representation fails.

---

## Beauty

Beauty is not the goal.

Beauty may emerge.

Fidelity comes first.

---

## One Line Principle

Fidelity before Beauty.
